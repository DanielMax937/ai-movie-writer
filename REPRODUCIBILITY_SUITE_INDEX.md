**A:** Start with `REPRODUCIBILITY_QUICK_START.md` - it's designed for immediate action.

### Q: How long does it take to achieve 75% reproducibility?

**A:** No! Use the template as a checklist. You'll naturally remember the Priority Top-20 after 2-3 uses.

**A:** That's okay! Document what you can. Aim for 60% minimum, 75% target. Use the worksheet to identify quick wins.

**A:** Check the Implementation Guide for 4-level quality scales. Generally: specific numbers = Excellent, general descriptions = Good.

**A:** Yes! The template is a starting point. Add project-specific sections, but keep the core 80-criteria checklist.

### Q: How often should I use this?
**A:** Every significant verification (pre-deployment, post-deployment, major releases).

### Q: What's the minimum passing score?
**A:** 60% overall, with Priority Top-20 at 90%+, and mandatory categories at 100%.

### Q: How do I improve from 70% to 85%?
**A:** Use the "Quick Win Recommendations" in the Scoring Worksheet. Usually 10-15 minutes of additional documentation.

### Q: Is this overkill for small projects?
**A:** Use the "First Pass" approach (5 minutes, 60%) from the Quick Start guide. Scale up as needed.
