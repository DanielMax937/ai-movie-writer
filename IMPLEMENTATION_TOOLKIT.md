```

## 2️⃣ POST-LAUNCH ENGAGEMENT SCRIPTS

### First 24 Hours - Critical Engagement Period

**HOUR 1-2: Launch Blitz**

```
LAUNCH SEQUENCE (Copy & Execute)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PUBLISH POST
   ⏰ Time: Tuesday, 10:00 AM PST (optimal)
   ✅ Action: Hit publish button

2. HACKER NEWS SUBMISSION (Within 5 minutes)
   ⏰ Time: 10:05 AM PST
   ✅ Title: [Exact post title - no changes]
   ✅ URL: [Your blog post URL]
   ✅ Text: Leave blank (link to post directly)
   
   Example title:
   "How We Document Software: A Bimodal Strategy"
   
   ❌ Don't use:
   - "We built an amazing..."
   - "X reasons why..."
   - Promotional language

3. TWITTER THREAD (Within 10 minutes)
   ⏰ Time: 10:10 AM PST
   ✅ Post thread (5-7 tweets)
   ✅ Pin first tweet
   ✅ Link to blog in final tweet
   
   Template:
   
   Tweet 1/5:
   "After 3 years of documentation chaos, we discovered something 
   surprising: teams don't need *better* docs. They need *two kinds* 
   of docs. 
   
   Here's why 👇"
   
   Tweet 2/5:
   "The problem: Most teams write 20-page docs that executives never 
   read, and one-pagers that engineers can't implement from.
   
   Both fail. Here's what works instead..."
   
   [Continue thread with key insights]
   
   Tweet 5/5:
   "Full breakdown with templates, examples, and ROI analysis:
   [Your blog URL]
   
   Bookmark this 🔖"

4. LINKEDIN POST (Within 15 minutes)
   ⏰ Time: 10:15 AM PST
   ✅ Post professional summary
   ✅ Tag relevant people/companies (optional)
   
   Template:
   
   "Documentation is broken in most organizations.
   
   After 3 years of experimentation, we've found a solution that 
   reduces reading time by 80% while improving comprehension by 3×.
   
   The key insight: Bimodal documentation architecture.
   
   Instead of one-size-fits-all docs, we create two versions:
   • 1-page quick reference (5 min read)
   • Complete deep-dive (20 min read)
   
   Results:
   ✓ 90% reduction in 'where do I find X?' questions
   ✓ 3× faster onboarding
   ✓ 95% team satisfaction
   
   Full methodology, templates, and lessons learned:
   [Your blog URL]
   
   #Documentation #Engineering #Productivity"

5. EMAIL TO EXISTING SUBSCRIBERS (Within 30 minutes)
   ⏰ Time: 10:30 AM PST
   ✅ Send from personal email (not automated)
   ✅ Keep it brief and personal
   
   Subject: New post: [Title]
   
   Body:
   "Hi [Name],
   
   I just published a new post about [topic]. 
   
   After 3 years of [problem], we discovered [key insight].
   
   This is our most comprehensive breakdown yet. It includes:
   • [Benefit 1]
   • [Benefit 2]
   • [Benefit 3]
   
   Read it here: [URL]
   
   Would love to hear your thoughts.
   
   Best,
   [Your Name]"

6. MONITOR & RESPOND (Continuous)
   ⏰ Time: 10:30 AM onwards
   ✅ Check HN comments every 15 minutes
   ✅ Respond within 30 minutes
   ✅ Be helpful, not defensive
```

## 4️⃣ SALES CALL SCRIPT TEMPLATE

### For Business Inquiries (30-Minute Call)

```
SALES CALL SCRIPT - 30 MINUTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OPENING (2 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time to chat.

I saw you reached out after reading my post on [topic]. 

Before we dive in, I want to make sure this is a good use of your time. 

Here's how I'd like to structure our call:
• Spend 10 minutes understanding your situation
• Spend 10 minutes exploring potential solutions
• Spend 5 minutes discussing next steps
• Leave 5 minutes for questions

Sound good?"

[Wait for agreement]

DISCOVERY (10 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ask these questions:

1. "What specifically caught your attention in the post?"
   → Listen for pain points

2. "Walk me through your current process for [topic]."
   → Identify workflow issues

3. "What have you tried so far to solve this?"
   → Understand past attempts

4. "What's the impact of this problem on your business?"
   → Quantify the pain (time, money, frustration)

5. "If you could wave a magic wand, what would the ideal solution look like?"
   → Clarify desired outcome

6. "What's your timeline for solving this?"
   → Establish urgency

7. "Who else is involved in this decision?"
   → Identify stakeholders

[Take notes on: Pain points, Budget signals, Timeline, Decision makers]

SOLUTION DISCUSSION (10 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Based on what you've shared, here are a few ways I might be able to help:

OPTION 1: [Consulting/Service]
• What it is: [Brief description]
• Timeline: [Duration]
• Investment: [Price range]
• Best for: [Use case]

OPTION 2: [Workshop/Training]
• What it is: [Brief description]
• Timeline: [Duration]
• Investment: [Price range]
• Best for: [Use case]

OPTION 3: [DIY with Templates]
• What it is: [Brief description]
• Timeline: [Duration]
• Investment: [Price range]
• Best for: [Use case]

Which of these resonates most with your situation?"

[Discuss chosen option]

NEXT STEPS (5 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Great. Here's what I suggest for next steps:

If you want to move forward:
1. I'll send you a detailed proposal by [date]
2. Review it with your team
3. We can schedule a follow-up call to discuss any questions
4. If everything looks good, we can start [timeframe]

Does that work for you?"

[If yes:]
"Perfect. I'll send that proposal over by [date]. 

In the meantime, here are a few resources that might be helpful:
• [Resource 1]
• [Resource 2]

Any questions before we wrap up?"

[If no:]
"No problem at all. Are there any specific concerns or questions I can address right now?"

CLOSING (3 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Thanks again for your time, [Name]. 

I'll send over:
• Call summary
• Proposal (if applicable)
• Relevant resources

Feel free to reach out anytime if questions come up.

Looking forward to [next step]!"

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
POST-CALL CHECKLIST:
[ ] Send thank-you email within 2 hours
[ ] Add to CRM/tracking spreadsheet
[ ] Send proposal within 24-48 hours (if applicable)
[ ] Schedule follow-up (if needed)
[ ] Update revenue pipeline estimate
```

## 8️⃣ COMMENT RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

### 5 Comment Types + Response Templates

**TYPE 1: Skeptical/Critical Comment**

```
Comment: "This won't work for large teams. Too simplistic."

Response Template:
"Great question about scalability!

You're right that [their concern] is a valid consideration for 
larger teams.

In our experience with teams of [size], we found that [solution].

Specifically:
• [Point 1]
• [Point 2]

What size team are you working with? I'd be curious to hear what 
challenges you're facing."

Key principles:
✅ Acknowledge their concern
✅ Provide data/experience
✅ Ask follow-up question
❌ Get defensive
❌ Dismiss their concern
```

---

**TYPE 2: Constructive/Adding Value**

```
Comment: "This is great. We also found that [additional insight]."

Response Template:
"Excellent addition!

I hadn't thought about [their insight] that way. That's a really 
smart approach.

Have you written about this anywhere? I'd love to read more about 
your experience with [topic]."

Key principles:
✅ Amplify their contribution
✅ Show genuine interest
✅ Build relationship
✅ Ask for more
```

---

**TYPE 3: Negative/Trolling**

```
Comment: "This is obvious. Why waste time writing this?"

Response Template:
"Thanks for reading!

While this might be obvious to experienced practitioners like yourself, 
we've found that [beginner audience] often struggles with [problem].

If you've mastered this, I'd be curious: What advanced topics would 
you like to see covered?"

Key principles:
✅ Stay professional
✅ Reframe for target audience
✅ Redirect constructively
❌ Engage in argument
❌ Get emotional
```

---

**TYPE 4: Requesting More Information**

```
Comment: "Can you share more about [specific topic]?"

Response Template:
"Absolutely! Great question.

[Brief answer to their question - 2-3 sentences]

I'm actually planning a follow-up post that dives deeper into this.
