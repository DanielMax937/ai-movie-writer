## 💡 **Timing Best Practices**

### **For 27-Slide Version:**
1. **Practice at least 3 times** to stay within 25 minutes
2. **Mark optional slides** you can skip if running long
3. **Use a timer** visible to yourself
4. **Prepare 20-min version** by identifying skip-able content
5. **Have backup** if demo fails (saves 2 minutes)

### **For 10-Slide Version:**
1. **Practice at least 2 times** to stay within 11.5 minutes
2. **Know your expansion points** if you have extra time
3. **Prepare compression version** for 8-minute scenario
4. **Memorize key statistics** for smooth delivery
5. **Use speaking notes** since slides are condensed
