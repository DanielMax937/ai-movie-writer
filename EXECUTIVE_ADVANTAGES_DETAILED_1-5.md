├─ Multiple example slides → one powerful example
├─ Redundant explanations → concise statements
└─ Nice-to-know details → need-to-know only

Retained (80% of content):
├─ Strategic vision and goals ✓
├─ Key achievements and metrics ✓
├─ Critical success factors ✓
├─ Risk assessment and mitigation ✓
├─ Financial impact and ROI ✓
├─ Clear next steps and decisions ✓
├─ Resource requirements ✓
└─ Timeline and milestones ✓

Result: Zero compromise on decision-making quality

├─ Can hold: 5-9 pieces of information
├─ Optimal load: 7±2 items (Miller's Law)
├─ Decision-making threshold: 5-7 key factors

27-Slide Cognitive Impact:
├─ Information pieces: 27-35 distinct points
